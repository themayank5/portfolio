Case Study · 02

Proptifi — Making AI-powered property visualization simple and accessible

I designed a guided AI visualization experience that helps homeowners, interior designers, and real-estate professionals explore property ideas faster—without relying on complex tools or repeated manual iterations.

Role:- UI/UX Designer Focus:- UX Strategy · User Research · UI Design · Prototyping Platform:- Web Application
01 — ABOUT THE PRODUCT

One platform. Five AI capabilities.

Proptifi brings multiple AI-powered visualization capabilities into one guided experience, helping users transform, explore, and iterate on property ideas from a single platform.

02 — THE PROBLEM

Traditional property visualization was slow, expensive, and difficult to explore.

Traditional visualization workflows were expensive, time-consuming, and difficult to iterate on. Users often had to rely on specialists for relatively simple visual changes, making experimentation slow and costly.

How might we make AI-powered property visualization simple enough for non-technical users while giving them the confidence to explore and iterate?
03 — RESEARCH & INSIGHTS

Understanding users and the market

I combined qualitative research, market analysis, and workflow evaluation to understand how users currently approach property visualization and where existing AI experiences fall short.

→

→

→

→
Feature Benchmarking

Key insight

Users didn't just need better AI generation. They needed a clearer way to decide what to generate, how to configure it, and what to do with the result.
04 — OPPORTUNITY

What the market was missing

Existing solutions offered powerful visualization capabilities, but the experience was fragmented across different tools, workflows, and interaction models.

01 — Fragmented experience
Users had to navigate between different tools and workflows depending on what they wanted to create.
02 — Multiple editing modes
Different AI capabilities often required different interaction patterns and inputs.
03 — Limited guidance
Users were expected to understand which tool and configuration would produce the desired result.
04 — Lack of property focus
Generic AI image tools weren't always designed around the specific needs of property visualization.

The opportunity

The opportunity wasn't to add more AI features. It was to make them feel like one coherent product experience.
05 — PRODUCT STRATEGY

From traditional visualization to self-service

We shifted the experience from a slow, one-directional process to a guided workflow that lets users generate, compare, and iterate independently.

06 — KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

Five decisions that shaped the product

The product experience was shaped around five decisions designed to reduce cognitive load, preserve context, and make AI feel approachable.

Decision 01
Upload-first onboarding
Decision: Start with the user's property image rather than asking them to configure the AI first.
Why: The image provides immediate context and makes the next step easier to understand.
Decision 02
Consistent AI workflow across all five tools
Decision: Use a shared interaction model across Fresh Look, Sky Color, Colors & Textures, Text-to-Image, and Precision+.
Why: Users can learn one workflow and apply that knowledge across different AI capabilities.
Decision 03
Before & after comparison
Decision: Allow users to compare generated results with the original image.
Why: Visual comparison helps users understand the transformation and builds confidence in the generated output.
Decision 04
Persistent navigation
Decision: Keep access to the AI tools, history, and account context available throughout the experience.
Why: Users can move between tasks without losing their place or context.
Decision 05
Progressive complexity
Decision: The core workflow remains simple (upload → generate) while advanced configuration options are introduced only when the user is ready for them.
07 — FINAL EXPERIENCE

Three-step user experience

The final experience reduced the core workflow to three understandable stages.

08 — OUTCOME

What changed?

01 — A guided self-serve experience

Transformed a fragmented visualization workflow into a structured digital experience.

02 — Simpler AI interaction

Made multiple AI capabilities easier to understand through a consistent workflow.

03 — Greater confidence in results

Comparison, history, and iterative generation helped users evaluate and refine outputs.

04 — A scalable product foundation

Established reusable patterns across AI capabilities while maintaining a consistent experience.
